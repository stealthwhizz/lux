# Brag Plan: Lux

## What is this app?
A self-hosted personal AI agent for everyone at a company: it keeps working when your laptop is closed, works in your own accounts, and asks before it acts.

## The angle
The agent's day doesn't end when yours does. Close the laptop; Lux is still on, on your company's own servers, and it still checks with you before anything leaves.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"You closed your laptop." then, on the music's first strong hit, "Your agent didn't." beside a GitClaw whose working ring keeps turning.

## Key moments (the middle)
- One click on + makes a "New agent"; it greets you with answer buttons, you pick "Plan our offsite", and it renames itself "Trip Planner".
- It wants to email the team; the orange Allow / Don't allow card appears and the avatar ring turns orange; Allow; "Sent."
- The Connectors dropdown: Google, Microsoft 365 and Slack connect one by one.

## Outro / punchline
"Lux. Always on. Asks first. Self-hosted." with "open source · MIT · every agent is a gitagent".

## User flow worth showing
Create an agent (+ → Create an agent) → give it a first job (answer button) → approve its action (Allow) → result in chat, plus connecting accounts.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: warm, confident product film for an enterprise tool that respects people's time
- Interpretation: few scenes with real holds, soft dips between scenes, narration calm and conversational, no hype words.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 23.4 s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: soft black #101010 (app paper #151514, surface #21201c)
- Accent: burnt orange #D5360C (text accent #f26a43)
- Text: warm ivory #eee7cf; wordmark ivory #E6D58D
- Display and body font: Instrument Sans Variable; mono: IBM Plex Mono
- Strongest visual element: the GitClaw mascots and their status rings; the app's own stylesheet drives every UI scene

## Share copy (draft)
Lux: a personal AI agent for everyone at your company, on your own servers. Close your laptop and it keeps working, in your Gmail, Outlook, Drive and Slack, and it asks before it sends anything.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed under narration, sparse motion-matched accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11 (bundled, 114.84 BPM)
- Music treatment: fades in, dips to about 0.13 under each narration line and lifts between them (the lift lands the reveal), fades under the final logo
- Music cue guidance: preset `assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`. Strong cues used: 1.60 s (hook line 2) and 3.70 s (wordmark). Beat grid 17.91 / 18.44 / 18.96 s for the three connectors.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; the background glow breathes with the music's energy. No waveform or pulsing text.
- SFX posture: sparse; soft impacts on the two locked reveals, a click per simulated cursor press, one quiet bell on the approval and on the logo, soft switches for connectors.
- Audio-coupled moments: cursor clicks, the approval, connectors connecting one by one.
- Restraint rule: nothing louder than the voice; no sound on every animation.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 0.0–3.7 s
"You closed your laptop." (0.35 s) then "Your agent didn't." in orange, locked to the 1.60 s hit, beside the orange GitClaw with a turning ring.
Sequential/interaction: two lines rise in one by one.
Audio intent: quiet start, soft impact on line 2. VO: "Six p.m. You've logged off."
Transition mood: soft dip → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Reveal — 3.7–8.4 s
"Lux" wordmark on the 3.70 s hit, nine GitClaws pop in left to right, tagline "A personal AI agent for everyone at your company." held about 2.8 s.
Sequential/interaction: mascots pop one by one.
Audio intent: the music lifts into the hit, then dips under "Meet Lux…".
Transition mood: soft dip → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — One click — 8.4–13.0 s
The app (real stylesheet). Cursor presses +, then Create an agent. "New agent" greets with three answer buttons; the cursor picks "Plan our offsite"; the name becomes "Trip Planner". Caption "One click. It names itself."
Sequential/interaction: yes: clicks, buttons appear one by one, the name changes.
Audio intent: soft clicks.
Transition mood: continuous → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Asks first — 13.0–16.8 s
"Searching your email", then the Allow / Don't allow card for an email to team@acme.com; ring turns orange; cursor presses Allow; "Sent. 40 people invited…". Caption "It asks before it acts."
Sequential/interaction: yes: approval click.
Audio intent: click and a quiet bell on approval.
Transition mood: the window steps back → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Connectors — 16.8–20.15 s
The Connectors dropdown, large: Google, Microsoft 365 and Slack switch to Connected on the beat grid. Caption "Your accounts. Your server." with "Gmail · Calendar · Drive · Outlook · OneDrive · Slack".
Sequential/interaction: three rows connect one by one.
Audio intent: three soft switches.
Transition mood: soft dip → Scene 6

### Scene 6 — Outro — 20.4–23.4 s
"Lux", "Always on. Asks first. Self-hosted.", "open source · MIT · every agent is a gitagent". Music fades.
