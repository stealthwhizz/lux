# Lux launch video: plan

**What it is:** a self-hosted personal AI agent for everyone at a company. Always on, asks before it acts.
**For:** companies that want Dots/Muse-style personal agents for every employee, on their own servers.
**Sets it apart:** runs on your server (keeps working when the laptop is closed), approvals before anything leaves, per-person connectors whose keys never reach the agent, open source.
**Most impressive claim:** close your laptop; your agent keeps working, and still asks before it sends anything.
**Visual hook:** "You closed your laptop." / "Your agent didn't." with a GitClaw whose status ring keeps orbiting.
**Real UI shown:** the app's own stylesheet, fonts (Instrument Sans, IBM Plex Mono), palette (soft black #101010, ivory #E6D58D, burnt orange #D5360C) and GitClaw mascots: sidebar, + menu, agent chat with answer buttons, self-naming, the Allow / Don't allow card, the Connectors dropdown.
**Tone:** polished with a little warmth (app-store pacing, soft transitions).
**Share caption:** Lux: a personal AI agent for everyone at your company, on your own server. It keeps working when your laptop is closed, and asks before it acts.
